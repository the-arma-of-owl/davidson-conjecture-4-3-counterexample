The monic reduction of \(F\) modulo \(3\) passes the degree-six irreducibility test. Since \(\alpha+\alpha^{-1}\) is real and \(\alpha\) is nonreal and quadratic over its trace field, \(\alpha\) has degree \(12\). Colour preservation gives divisibility by \(3\), and a shortest grid path gives both the parity congruence and the \(\ell_1\) bound. |
